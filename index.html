Early Career & Mainstream Media

Print & Digital Foundation

Former Regional Writer

Daily Nation Publication

Before establishing himself as a prominent digital brand, Mohamed built a foundational career in traditional media. He served as a regional writer for the Daily Nation, one of East Africa's largest print and digital news publications.

During his tenure with mainstream media, he extensively covered localized issues, communal events, and regional administration challenges in Kenya's frontier counties. This foundational period provided him with deep institutional knowledge of the clan-based political dynamics, governance hurdles, and structural issues native to the Northern corridor.

Pillar 01

Politics & Governance

Mohamed is highly recognized for dissecting regional political frameworks. He closely tracks electoral shifts, gubernatorial contests, and legislative developments across local constituencies. Notably, his coverage includes independent analysis of prominent regional campaign trails—such as the ALFA009 political movement and major endorsements within the United Democratic Movement (UDM).

Pillar 02

Socio-Economics & Accountability

A core aspect of Mohamed’s profile is his focus on financial transparency. Writing and broadcasting under his Shap Papaz moniker, he acts as a public watchdog monitoring county executive performance, devolution allocations, and local public service delivery—frequently inspecting facilities like the Rhamu Sub-County Hospital.

Pillar 03

National Security & Transnational Issues

Operating from strategically sensitive Mandera, Mohamed provides field reporting on border stability, inter-clan tensions, and national security directives. He investigates human trafficking mechanisms and the hazardous "Tahrib" smuggling corridor impacting East African youth.
